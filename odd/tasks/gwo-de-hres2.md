Records per run: policy, signal, seed, objective, feasible, physical LCOE/LCOH/AGSR, solution, decoded capacities, evaluations, series, changes, elapsed. CLI writes `runs.jsonl` + `manifest.json` to a fresh directory and refuses a non-empty one.
